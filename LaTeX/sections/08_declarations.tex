\section*{Code and Data Availability}
Source code, experiment scripts, compact result tables, and the manuscript are
available at \url{https://github.com/EthyleneC2H4/NUS-Capstone}. Trained model

\url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7654890}). MSigDB Hallmark 2020 gene sets were
obtained from \url{https://www.gsea-msigdb.org}.
